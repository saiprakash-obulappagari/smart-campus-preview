Student Success Score

A transparent support index from 0 to 100. Risk flags are decision support, not validated probabilities of failure.

Calculation

Academic combines CGPA × 10 at 35%, marks at 50%, and subject performance at 15%. Subtract 5 points per backlog, capped at 25. Other categories use the mean of available indicators.

Success = sum(category score × category weight) / sum(available category weights). Missing values are excluded. Required-field coverage is reported separately. Attendance-only evidence at 90% gives a partial score of 90 with 15% coverage.

Explainability

Contribution points show how much each category adds to success. Shortfall points show the gap to 100 from recorded evidence. The dashboard exposes each risk trigger, rule, value, source, and timestamp.

Risk rules

Default percentage thresholds: below 30 high, 30–59.99 medium. Backlogs ≥1 medium and ≥3 high. Latest dated marks declines ≥5 points medium and ≥10 high. Placement checks coding, aptitude, and interviews. No observed indicators means unknown risk.

Data integrity

Faculty verify source references before importing CSV or JSON. Invalid values, malformed dates, duplicates, and out-of-scope IDs are rejected. Dated data updates per field; older records stay in history. Undated legacy records are excluded from trends. Check coverage before comparing dates.

Human-guided support

Segments support targeted action. Faculty approve tasks and record follow-up evidence. Task completion alone does not prove improvement. This prototype uses explainable rules and does not claim machine-learning prediction accuracy.
